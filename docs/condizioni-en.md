## 1. The program

QTHome is a program for radio amateurs for Mac and Windows. It is a free download. Without a licence you can listen, decode and keep an SWL log; CW decoding stays free forever. Transmitting, automatic functions, advanced contesting, electronic QSLs and messaging between hams need the PRO licence. The first 30 days are a full trial, without paying and without giving payment details.

## 2. The PRO licence

- It costs €12 a year, VAT included where due, paid with PayPal on the shop page. It does not renew by itself: when it expires the PRO functions stop and the rest keeps working.
- It is personal, tied to your callsign, and valid on one computer at a time. If you change computer, write to us and we free it.

## 3. Your station, your responsibility

QTHome transmits only when you ask it to or when you turn on an automatic function. You, as the holder of the amateur radio licence, are responsible for what goes on the air: frequencies, power, modes, times and the rules of your country. Automatic functions (for example automatic replies in Digital modes) must be used only where they are allowed and while staying near the station. QTHome does not replace the protections of your station: always use fuses, compliant antennas and, for tests, a dummy load.

## 6. Privacy

QTHome works on your computer. There are no ads, no hidden usage statistics, and we sell no data to anyone.

**What stays only on your computer.** Log, settings, recordings, received images and service passwords (LoTW, QRZ, EchoLink, Winlink and others) stay in the QTHome folder; passwords are in files readable only by your user.

**What leaves, and why.**

- **Purchase**: callsign and email, to create and send you the licence. PayPal handles the payment; we never see card details. The email is sent by Brevo. Order and licence are stored on our service (Cloudflare) for the time required by tax law.
- **Activation**: the licence code and a random installation number, to enforce "one licence, one computer". It contains neither your name nor computer data.
- **Updates and WSJT-X programs**: QTHome downloads new versions from GitHub. As with any website, GitHub sees the IP address of the request.
- **Reports**: only when you press "Report a problem or an idea…". Your text, the section of the program you were in, the radio model and the locator (at most 6 characters) are sent; before leaving, the text goes through a filter that removes user and computer names, personal folders, emails, network addresses, passwords and keys, and replaces callsigns with a code.
- **Services you choose to use**: DX cluster, Reverse Beacon Network, PSK Reporter, HamQTH, LoTW, eQSL, QRZ, Club Log, HRDLog, APRS-IS, EchoLink, Winlink, POTA/SOTA/WWFF spots, RepeaterBook, propagation data (hamqsl.com, NOAA, KC2G/GIRO), the country list (country-files.com), Super Check Partial and network time. QTHome contacts them only for the functions you turn on, with the data that function needs (usually your callsign, sometimes your locator or the QSOs to upload). Each has its own privacy rules.
- **QTHome Chat**: messages pass through our service and are not stored; private messages and voice calls are end-to-end encrypted.
- **Cloud log** (only if you turn it on): a copy of your log is kept on our service (Cloudflare), separate for each callsign. Only someone with your licence or the club key you created can read it. It is not encrypted, so it opens in a browser: technically whoever runs the service could see it, but we neither look at it nor use it. Turning it off stops updates; to delete the copy, write to us.

**Basis and rights.** We process purchase data to give you the licence (contract) and for tax obligations; reports because you send them. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by writing to us; deletion cannot cover what tax law requires us to keep. You can also contact the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali) or the one in your country.
